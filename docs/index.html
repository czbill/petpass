Private by design

No account, no cloud

Your pet's records, photos and backups stay on your device. There is no PetPass server storing them, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Backups you control

Export a zip backup whenever you like and restore it on a new phone. You decide where it goes.

Honest about scope

PetPass organizes records you enter yourself. It is not a veterinary diagnosis or an official medical or travel certificate.
